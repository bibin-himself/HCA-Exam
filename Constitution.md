`/// Partly Flexible & Partly Rigid \\\`

Neither too easy nor too difficult to amend
Amendments through different procedures
`Article 368 Part XX` deals with `amendment process` (2 types)

 
> 3 ways of Amendment:
  1. By Simple majority                     -  50%+ of members present and voting
  2. By Special majority                    -  50%+ of total membership of each House & at least `2/3` of members present and voting
  3. Special Majority + State Ratification  -  Special majority in both Houses & ratification by at least 50% of State Legislatures


> Simple Majority	-
	Formation of new States (Articles 2–4), creation or abolition of Legislative Councils (Article 169)

> Special Majority -
 	Fundamental Rights, DPSPs and most constitutional amendments under Article 368

> Special Majority + State Ratification	-
  Amendments affecting federal provisions, like Election of the President, Distribution of legislative powers and representation of States in Parliament

3. THE CRITICAL REASONING (WHY & HOW)

1. Why is the Indian Constitution so lengthy?
Because India is highly diverse in language, religion, culture, geography, and administration. Detailed provisions reduce ambiguity and conflict.

2. Why borrow from other constitutions?
The framers adopted the best features from successful constitutional systems worldwide instead of reinventing everything.

3. Why combine Federal and Unitary features?
India needed:
State autonomy (Federalism)
National unity and strong central control during crises (Unitarism)

4. Why choose Parliamentary Government?
To ensure that the executive remains accountable to the elected legislature and people.

5. Why have both Fundamental Rights and DPSPs?
Fundamental Rights protect citizens from excessive state action.
DPSPs guide the state in building a welfare society.

6. Why an Independent Judiciary?
To protect the Constitution, Fundamental Rights, and maintain the balance of power.

7. Why Single Citizenship?
To strengthen national integration and prevent regional divisions.

8. Why Emergency Provisions?
To allow swift and effective action when the nation faces serious threats or crises.
