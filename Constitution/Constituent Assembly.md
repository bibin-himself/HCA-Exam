

`///// ORIGIN \\\\\`
 
 Constitution framed by The Constituent Assembly

 `M.N. Roy` aka `Architect of People's Planning in India` proposed the idea of CA in 1934. (Indian Patriot)
  - Founder of Indian Communist Party, Mexican Communist Party, Radical Democratic Party (Mouthpiece - Vanguard)
  - Book written : India in Transition

 First political party demanded COI - `Swaraj Party`
  - Formed 1 Jan 1923
  - President - C.R. Das
  - Secretary - Motilal Nehru

 INC `Approved` it in `1936` at `Faizpur` Session
  - Chairman : Jawaharlal Nehru

 > Constitution Assembly formed in `1946 December 6`
   First Meeting - ```1946 DECEMBER 9```
   Held in Constitution Hall (now Central Hall of Parliament)
   `11` sessions
   `165` days
   `114` days for discussing `Draft Constitution`

 `// CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY: CHRONOLOGY //`
 
    01 Jan 1923	     : Swaraj Party Founded (C.R. Das & Motilal Nehru)

    1934	           : M.N. Roy proposed the idea of a Constituent Assembly
    1936	           : Faizpur Session approved the demand for a Constituent Assembly

    23 Mar 1946	     : Cabinet Mission arrived in India
    16 May 1946	     : Cabinet Mission Plan announced

    06 Dec 1946	     : Constituent Assembly formed
    09 Dec 1946	     : First Meeting of Constituent Assembly
    11 Dec 1946	     : Dr. Rajendra Prasad elected President
    13 Dec 1946	     : Objective Resolution introduced by Jawaharlal Nehru
    22 Jan 1947	     : Objective Resolution Approved/adopted

    22 Jul 1947	     : National Flag adopted

    29 Aug 1947	     : Drafting Committee formed
    30 Aug 1947      : Ambedkar Elected as chairman

    21 Feb 1948	     : Draft Constitution submitted to Assembly President
    28 Feb 1948	     : Draft Constitution published
    26 Nov 1949	     : Constitution adopted/approved
    24 Jan 1950	     : Final Session; National Anthem adopted; Vande Mataram as National Song; Members signed on Constitution
    26 Jan 1950	     : Constitution came into force; National Symbol adopted
