

`////  CITIZENSHIP - PART II: Art.5 to Art.11  \\\\`
 Single Citizenship
 Part II: Articles 5-11
 No dual citizenship like USA (No seperate National & State citizenship) 
 Allegiance only to Union of India

`\\Important Articles//` 
 > Article 5
    Citizenship commencement of Constitution - 26 Jan 1950

 > Article 6
    Migrants from Pakistan to India
  
 > Article 7
    Persons migrated to Pakistan who later returned

 > Article 8
    Persons of Indian Origin living abroad

 > Article 9
    Foreign citizenship acquired voluntarily - Indian citizenship not retained

 > Article 10
    Continuance of citizenship rights

 > Article 11
    Parliament can regulate citizenship by law


*  Arti. 5–8  - citizenship at the commencement of the Constitution
*  Arti. 9–11 - related general provisions.




`// Citizenship Act - 1955 \\`
 Passed under `Article 11`
 Amended several times

 > 5 modes of acquring Citizenship (BDRNI)
 1. Birth
    Based on birth in India, legal conditions applicable

 2. Descent
    Through Indian parentage, subject to legal conditions
 
 3. Registration
    Certain categories can register themselves

 4. Naturalisation
    Foreigner after satisfying presc. conditions, inc. residence requirements

 5. Incorporation of Territory
    When a foreign territory becomes part of India




`// OCI \\`
 Overseas Citizen of India
 Recommended by `L.M. Singhvi Committee`
  To strengthen India's relationship with it's diaspora
 Not a full Indian Citizenship

> Eligibility
  1. Was an Indian Citizen `after commencement of Constitution`
  2. Was eligible to become an Indian citizen `on 26 January 1950`
  3. Belonged to `territory` that become `part` of India `after 15 Aug 1947`
  4. Child/Grandchild/Great-grandchild of such persons

> Not Eligible
  Citizen of Pakistan & Bangladesh

> OCI Cannot
  1. Vote in elections
  2. Contest elections
  3. Hold Constitutional posts (President, Vice-President, Governor, Judge)
  4. Goverment employee




`// Citizenship Amendment Act (CAA) - 2019 \\`
 Relaxed pathway for specified religious minorities from 3 countries
  1. Religions    : Hindus, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains, Parsis, Christians
  2. Countries    : Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Pakisthan
  3. Cut-off date : Entered India on or before 31 December 2014
 > They shall not be treated as illegal migrants 



`/// NRC \\\`
 National Register of Citizens
 1. Register containing details of `Indian citizens`
 2. To identify genuine citizens
 3. Persons excluded → Illegal migrant
      


`/// NPC \\\`
 National Population Register
 1. Register of `usual residents` of the country
 2. Includes both `citizens` & `non-citizens`
 3. Usual resident: Resident for atleast 6 months










`////  FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS - PART III – ARTICLES 12 to 35 \\\\`
 Part III  Article 12-35
 Inspired by:
    Magna Carta    : England
    Bill of Rights : USA
 `Justiciable` - Enforceable in court
 Not absolute  - Subject to `reasonable restrictions` imposed by the `State`.

 Architecture of Fundamental Rights - `Sardar Vallabhai Patel`
 Protector                          - `Supreme Court of India`
 Weapon of Protection               - `Writs`


`\\Important Articles//` 
> Article 12 - `Definition of State`
  1. State Includes:
      * Government & Parliament of India
      * Government & Legislature of each state
      * Local authorities : Panchayats, Municipalities, District Boards, etc
      * Other statutory bodies & gov-controlled agencies

  Unlike most Fundamental Rights, `Articles 15(2), 17, 23, & 24` apply `against` both the `State and private` individuals


> Article 13 - `Judicial Review`
  1. Laws violating FR are void
  2. Forms an important consti. basis for Judicial Review
  3. Basis of Judicial review:
      * Article 13  : Laws violating FR  (for challenging laws that violate FR)
      * Article 32  : Supreme Court
      * Article 226 : High Courts


`Right to Equality (Articles 14–18)`

> Article 14 - `Equality before Law`
  Guarantees:
   1. Equality before law - No person is above the law
   2. Equal protection of the laws - Similar treatment in similar circumstances
  Available to all persons, not only citizens
  `Equality Before Law + Equal Protection of Laws`


> Article 15 - `Prohibition of Discrimination`
  1. Shall not discriminate against citizens on grounds only of:
      * Religion   
      * Race   
      * Caste   
      * Sex   
      * Place of birth
  2. Special Provisions:
      * Women & children
      * Socially & Educationally Backward Classes (SEBCs)
      * Scheduled Castes (SCs)
      * Scheduled Tribes (STs)
      * Economically Weaker Sections (EWS)


> Article 16 - `Equality of Oppurtunity in Public Employemnt`
  1. Mandal Commission - 1979
     * Recommended `27% OBC reservation` in govt. jobs
     * Appointed by the govt. led by `Morarji Desai`
  
  2. Indra Sawhney Case - 1992
     * Aka Mandal Commission Case
     * Upheld 27% OBC reservation
     * Excluded creamy layer
     * Established 50% reservation ceiling; subject to exceptional circumstances
     * No reservation in promotions

  3. 103rd Constitutional Amendment Act 2019
     * Introduced 10% EWS reservation
     * Amended:
        <Article 15> - EWS reservation in education
        <Article 16> - EWS reservation in public employment


> Article 17 - `Abolition of Untouchability`
  1. Abolished untouchability
  2. Its practice in any form is forbidden
  3. Punishable offence


> Article 18 - `Abolition of Titles`
  State cannot confer titles
  Exceptions: Military & Academic distinctions
  Indian citizens cannot accept title/gift/office from foreign states without President's consent
  `National Awards are not titles`
      : Bharat Ratna, Padma Vibushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri  




`Right to Freedom (Articles 19–22)`

> Article 19 - `Six Freedoms` 
  Available only to Indian citizens
  1. 19(1)(a)   : Speech & Expression.  (Incl. freedom of the press & the right to silence.)
  2. 19(1)(b)   : Peaceful Assembly without Arms
  3. 19(1)(c)   : Form Associations, Political parties, Unions & Cooperative Societies
  4. 19(1)(d)   : Move freely throughout India
  5. 19(1)(e)   : Reside & settle anywhere in India
  6. 19(1)(f)   : Practise any profession or carry on Occupation, Trade or Business
 
 * All 6 freedoms are subject to constitutionally permitted reasonable restrictions.    

  `97th Amendment (2011)`
    * Cooperative societies included under Arti. 19(1)(c)
    * Arti. 43B added DPSP for promoting cooperative societies
    * Part IXB - provisions related to cooperative societies


> Article 20 - `Protection in Criminal Cases`
  Protection in Respect of Conviction for Offences
  Provides 3 protections:
   1. Against Ex-post Facto law
       `No punishment` under a law that was `not in force` when the `act` was `committed`.
       `No greater penalty` than the one `applicable at the time` of the `offence`.
   2. Against double jeopardy
       `No` person shall be prosecuted & `punished` for the same offence `more than once`
   3. Against Self-incrimination
       `No` accused person shall be compelled to be a `witness against himself`



> Article 21 - `Protection of Life & Liberty`
  "No person shall be deprived of life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law." - Constitional wording

  * Available to all persons
  * Protects life and personal liberty
  * Includes several rights recogonised by the supreme Court through judicial interpretation

  `\\ Landmark Cases //`
    1. A.K. Gopalan Case (1950)
        * Upheld Procedure Established by Law
        * Adopted a narrow interpretation of personal liberty
    2. Maneka Gandhi Case (1978)
        * Expanded the interpretation of Article 21
        * Procedure must be just, fair & reasonable
        * Brought `Due Process of Law` into Indian constitutional interpretation

  `\\ Rights derived from Art. 21 //`
    1. Right to Privacy  -  K.S. Puttuswamy Case (2017)
    2. Right to go abroad
    3. Right to shelter
    4. Right to health
    5. Right to a clean environment
    6. Right to livelihood

  
> Article 21A - `Right to Education`
  1. Added by `86th` Constitutional `Amendment` Act, 2002
  2. Free & compulsory education to `children 6-14`
  3. RTE Act `enacted in 2009`
  4. RTE Act came into force `1 April 2010`



> Article 22 - `Protection against arrest and detention`
  * Rights of an arrested person
     1. Informed of the `grounds of arrest`
     2. `Consult` & be defended by a legal `practioner`
     3. Produced before a `Magistrate` within `24 hrs` of arrest
          Excl. the time of journey to Magistrate court
     4. Cannot be detained `beyond 24 hrs` without Magistrate's authority

  * Exceptions
     1. Enemy Aliens
     2. Persons detained under preventive detention laws

     Preventive Detention
      1. Detention without trial to prevent from commiting certain acts
      2. Detention beyond 3 months - Require opinion of Advisory Board
